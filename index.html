About

I read data the way a business asks questions.

My work sits at the intersection of analytics and business decision-making. I spend most of my time inside real, messy datasets — retail transactions, financial records, customer behavior logs — pulling out the signal that actually changes how a team acts.

That means cleaning and validating data before I trust a single number, running exploratory analysis to understand what's really happening, writing SQL to answer specific business questions, and building Power BI dashboards that stakeholders can actually use without a data background.

I'm equally comfortable in a Jupyter notebook running a churn model in Python and in a boardroom explaining why Q4 revenue spiked 30 percent. The goal is always the same: turn a dataset into a decision someone can act on.
